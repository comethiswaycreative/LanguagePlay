Privacy Policy

Come This Way: Language Play by Come This Way with CJ, South Africa.

What we collect

We do not collect, sell or share any personal information. We have no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What stays on your device

Your child's name (if you type it), the language you choose, your stamps, progress, words you have marked as taught, and any voice recordings you make are saved only on your own device, inside the app. We never receive them. Clearing your browser data or deleting the app removes them.

Microphone

The app asks to use your microphone only when you tap "Record my voice". Recordings are stored on your device only and you can delete them at any time with the delete button.

Links

The app links to our shop on Payhip (payhip.com) and to articles by other organisations. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is designed for parents to use together with their children. It does not ask children for any information.
